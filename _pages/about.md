I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the HKUST NLP Group at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, supervised by Professor Junxian He.

## Research areas and skills

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning. My research interests include:

- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning.
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM).
- LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability.

## Academic background

- **Ph.D. in Computer Science, 2024–Present** — Hong Kong University of Science and Technology.
- **B.Eng., 2020–2024** — Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), graduated in June 2024.

Professor Junxian He also advised me during my undergraduate studies at SJTU.
